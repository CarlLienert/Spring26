3.

The hint follows from question 1a).

If \(n\) is even, let \(n=2m\):

\[\begin{align*} t_1+t_2+\dots+t_n&= (t_1+t_2)+(t_3+t_4)+\dots+(t_{n-1}+t_n)\\ \\ &=2^2+4^2+\dots+(2m)^2\\ \\ &=2^2(1+2^2+3^2+\dots+m^2)\\ \\ &=2^2\cdot \left(\frac{m(2m+1)(m+1)}{6}\right)\\ \\ &=\frac{2m(2m+1)(2m+2)}{6}\\ \\ &=\frac{n(n+1)(n+2)}{6} \end{align*}\]

where we have used the result from section 1.1 concerning the sum of the first \(n\) squares.

If \(n\) is odd:

\[\begin{align*} t_1+t_2+\dots+t_n&= (t_1+t_2)+(t_3+t_4)+\dots+(t_{n-2}+t_{n-1})+t_n\\ \\ &=\frac{(n-1)(n)(n+1)}{6}+\frac{n(n+1)}{2}\\ \\ &=\frac{(n-1)(n)(n+1)+3n(n+1)}{6}\\ \\ &=\frac{(n+1)(n^2-n+3n)}{6}\\ \\ &=\frac{(n+1)(n)(n+2)}{6} \end{align*}\]

10.

Use induction. The key algebraic step is:

\[\begin{align*} \frac{(n-1)(3(n-1)-1)}{2}+(3n-2)&=\frac{(n-1)(3n-4)}{2}+(3n-2)\\ \\ &=\frac{(n-1)(3n-4)+2(3n-2)}{2}\\ \\ &=\frac{3n^2-n}{2}\\ \\ &=\frac{n(3n-1)}{2} \end{align*}\]
